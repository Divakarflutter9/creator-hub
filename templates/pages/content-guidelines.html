Content guidelines

Creators must have the rights and permissions needed to upload and share every resource and article they publish.

Do not publish illegal material, copyright-infringing works, malware, deceptive downloads, or content that promotes harm, harassment, or abuse. Uploads may be disabled while a report is reviewed.

Report a resource

Use “Report this resource” on its public page, or email {{ support_email }} with the URL and reason. We review reports and may restrict or remove content that violates these guidelines or applicable policies.

Creator responsibility

Creators are responsible for their articles, files, permissions, and claims. CreatorHub may remove content or restrict accounts to protect visitors and comply with applicable requirements.
