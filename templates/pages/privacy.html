Privacy policy

CreatorHub is operated by Divakar Kolli in India. This page explains the information used to run the service.

Information we handle

We handle account names and email addresses, password hashes, resource titles and descriptions, uploaded files, resource view/download counts, reports, and information needed to operate and secure accounts. Necessary browser cookies support sessions and security. Server and security logs may include technical information such as IP address, request time, and browser details.

How information is used

We use this information to provide accounts, store and deliver resources, show creators basic activity counts, respond to reports and support requests, and protect the service from abuse. Publicly published resource pages and their articles/files can be accessed by anyone with the link.

Storage and service providers

CreatorHub stores its application data and private uploads on its VPS. Email is sent through the configured email provider (currently Gmail if Gmail SMTP is enabled). These providers process information as needed to host the service or deliver messages.

Retention and deletion requests

Account information and resources are kept while an account is active. Email {{ support_email }} to request deletion or ask about your information. We may retain limited security, abuse, legal, and backup records where necessary; backup copies may persist until separately removed.

Cookies and advertising

CreatorHub currently uses necessary session and security cookies. If advertising is enabled, Google and other advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads. Users can manage Google personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings. Before enabling ads, we will update this notice to reflect the actual ad technology and provide consent choices where required.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email {{ support_email }}.
